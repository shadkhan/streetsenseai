CAPABILITIES

Three gaps in the UK roadworks stack — closed.

Cross-authority corridor risk scoring

Permit officers approve works every day without seeing what else is happening on the same corridor — across authority boundaries, across utility companies. StreetSense AI scores cumulative disruption impact in real time. The DfT's equivalent ("Flagging high impact works") is listed as Potential focus with no delivery date.

DfT Status: Potential focus · No date

Plain-English AI copilot

Network coordinators spend hours cross-referencing permits, traffic orders, and historical data to answer one question. Ask StreetSense AI in plain English — "What's happening on the A38 next month?" — and get a grounded answer with cited permit references. The DfT's alerting feature ("Where next") is scheduled for September 2027.

DfT Status: Phase 15 · September 2027

National non-compliance benchmarking

Highway authorities have no visibility into how their permit promoters compare nationally. Which utility companies systematically overrun? Which generate the most FPN opportunities? StreetSense AI benchmarks all 1,800+ active promoters across England. Cross-authority coordination is explicitly out of scope for Street Manager.

DfT Status: Out of scope — permanently
